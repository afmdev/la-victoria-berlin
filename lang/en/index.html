Our Story

STRAIGHT FROM LIMA'S STREETS

La Victoria brings real Peruvian street food to Berlin — not the upscale restaurant version, but the original. Anticuchos from the charcoal grill, fresh Ceviche with rocoto chilli, crispy Papa Rellena: dishes that have been sold on the streets of Lima for generations.

The scent of charcoal, the sizzle of the grill, the warmth of a homemade Salsa Criolla — that is Peruvian street food. No trend, no fusion: just the recipes every Peruvian knows and loves.

We are at Markthalle Pfefferberg, Prenzlauer Berg. The grill is always smoking.
